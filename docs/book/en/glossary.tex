Term & Meaning and location in this book \\
\midrule
\endhead
Append & Write records at the log tail using consecutive offsets; Chapters 1, 2, and 6.\\
Offset & A logical record number in a partition log, not a file byte position; Chapter 1.\\
Byte position & The physical starting position of a record in one segment file; Chapters 1 and 2.\\
LEO & Log End Offset, the offset of the next record that can be written; Chapters 1 and 6.\\
HW & High Watermark, the committed boundary visible to ordinary consumers; Chapters 6 and 7.\\
ISR & In-Sync Replicas, the set of replicas that satisfy this course’s synchronization conditions with the current leader; Chapters 6 and 7.\\
Generation & A version assigned to consumer-group membership, used to reject commits from stale members; Chapter 5.\\
Leader epoch & A version of the partition leader role; requests from the previous role are fenced after a transition; Chapter 7.\\
At-least-once & Processing may be repeated, but records that were not successfully processed are not silently skipped before commit; Chapter 4.\\
